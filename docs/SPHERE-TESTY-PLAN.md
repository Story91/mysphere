W praktyce najbardziej prawdopodobna porazka to nie kontrakty, tylko ekonomia zimnego startu: jedyna wymuszona lancuchem presja kupna SPHERE w fazie 0 to ~0.35% ETH-wolumenu SAMEGO SPHERE po graduacji (buybackBps=5000 tnie 50% z 70% dzialki tworcy z 1% fee hooka); cala reszta petli "wolumen padu -> buyback SPHERE" wisi na uznaniowej dyscyplinie multisigu do fazy 2/3, pule epok sa ograniczone realnym przychodem (zero emisji z konstrukcji, dev-buy wylaczony z finansowania sezonow), a plynnosc SPHERE na starcie (~2.9 ETH w puli) jest plytka. Do tego snajperzy na przewidywalnym vanity adresie wezma istotna czesc wczesnej podazy - spec to akceptuje, ale to oni beda pierwsza presja sprzedazy.

- **UX**: TAK - da sie to zrobic zrozumiale, bo 80% mechaniki mapuje sie 1:1 na wzorce, ktore startpad juz ma: kumulatywny Merkle claim to z perspektywy uzytkownika JEDEN przycisk "Claim rewards" (prostszy niz obecny claimAllMine, ktory robi 3 transakcje - tu jest jedna na wszystkie zalegle tygodnie); epoki to po prostu "Week #N" z odliczaniem; buyback/burn da sie pokazac tym samym segmentowym paskiem, ktorym modal launchu juz dziś tlumaczy pool fee split. ### `test/SphereRewards.test.ts`

Unit. The passive pull-hub in isolation plus as a launchpad fee receiver. Local launchpad via deployLaunchpad + installAt mocks only for the receiver-slot cases; pure-ETH cases need no launchpad.

- accepts bare call{value}('') from an EOA (the exact _sendEth primitive: assert balance delta == amount, tx succeeds)
- accepts bare call{value} from a CONTRACT (deploy a 1-line forwarder probe; proves delivery from launchpad/hook/vault code paths)
- accepts ETH via .transfer / .send (2300 gas stipend) - pins that receive() writes no storage, i.e. stays a plain accumulator forever
- accepts repeated deposits; balance strictly accumulates; NO outbound transfer ever occurs on receive (assert rewards.balance == sum of deposits after N sends - the 'zero forwarding' doctrine from spec section 8: a forwarder to a not-yet-existing staking would brick SPHERE forever)
- zero-value call{value:0} behavior pinned (accept as no-op - must not revert, because _sendEth is never called with 0 by the pad but third parties may probe)
- as a launchpad fee receiver: launchAdvanced with receiverAccounts=[rewards,escrow] and RECEIVER_SHARES_BPS; after a buy, pad.claimable(rewards) == creatorCut * shares[0]/10000 computed from the Bought event feeEth (formula from _accrueFee: creatorCut = mulDivDown(fee, creatorFeeBps, feeBps), last receiver gets remainder)
- claimFor(rewards) delivers: balance delta == claimable, claimable zeroed, Claimed event; caller is a third-party signer (permissionless delivery)
- pull-hub accounting: an authorized later contract PULLS an amount and only its amount; an unauthorized address cannot pull; hub never pushes [PARAMETRIZED - see blockers: puller-authorization model undecided; write the describe block with this.skip() and a TODO naming the blocker]
- immutability negative checks: contract exposes NO admin surface - assert typechain interface has no owner/transferOwnership/withdraw/set*/pause fragments (iterate rewards.interface.fragments); calling random selectors via raw call reverts or hits fallback without state change
- griefing: selfdestruct-forced ETH (SelfDestructDonator probe) does not break any accounting invariant (pull-hub reads its own book, not address(this).balance - same doctrine as hook receive())

### `test/SphereCreatorEscrow.test.ts`

Unit. The escrow in isolation against a locally deployed launchpad (deployLaunchpad helper, MockB20Factory + MockPoolManager installed). Proves escrow-as-creator, dev-buy under windowCap, ETH acceptance, the hard-coded release rule, reentrancy, immutability. Needs a new probes file contracts/mocks/SphereProbes.sol: ReentrantReleaseTarget (re-enters release() from receive), RejectingTarget (reverts on receive - EthRejector pattern), plus reuse of existing EthRejector.

- constructor pins immutables: LAUNCHPAD, target addresses, split constants readable and equal to constructor args; reverts on zero addresses and on split != 10000
- launch() calls launchVanity and the escrow BECOMES the creator: Launched event creator arg == escrow address; pad.launchOf(token) creator field == escrow; launchFeeBps/launchCreatorFeeBps/hook buybackBps (after graduation) match SPHERE_PARAMS
- predictVanityToken(escrowAddress, VANITY_SALT) == token address actually created (the CurveLaunchpadVanity.test.ts parity case, but with the CONTRACT as creator - the preimage is keccak(pad, creator, salt, chainid), so prediction must be done against the escrow's address, not the deployer's)
- same salt, different caller: predictVanityToken(eoa, salt) != predictVanityToken(escrow, salt) - front-running the escrow's published salt cannot steal its address
- dev-buy inside the launch tx: escrow.launch{value: DEV_BUY}; assert escrow token balance == tokensOut from the Bought event, windowBought(token, escrowAddress) == tokensOut - THE CAP BINDS THE ESCROW ADDRESS (CurveLaunchpad.sol:1129-1136: dev-buy goes through the same _buy with msg.sender == escrow)
- windowCap atomicity: with WINDOW_CAP sized below the dev-buy's tokensOut, the ENTIRE launch tx reverts WindowCap - nothing partial, no token created (spec section 4: cap must be sized FOR the dev-buy; this test is the executable form of that rule)
- one-shot: second launch() call reverts (launched flag); config is baked - no caller-supplied receivers/shares/bps can be injected at call time
- receiver slot: launch passes receiverAccounts=[rewards, escrow], shares=SPHERE_PARAMS.RECEIVER_SHARES_BPS; after third-party buys, pad.claimable(escrow) matches the share formula
- claimFor(escrow) delivery: third-party signer calls pad.claimFor(escrow); escrow balance delta == claimable; proves receive() accepts the bare call from launchpad bytecode
- plain-ETH receive: EOA send and contract-forwarder send both accepted (same primitive matrix as SphereRewards)
- release rule: fund escrow with a known amount, permissionless release() splits per RELEASE_SPLIT_BPS between the two immutable targets; legs sum to the total TO THE WEI; dust (odd wei) goes to the documented remainder leg (repo rounding doctrine: smaller side mulDivDown, other side gets the rest)
- release with 1 wei: no revert, all to remainder leg; release with 0 balance: reverts NothingToRelease (pin: empty tx must not look like a payout - same doctrine as _payout)
- reentrancy on release: ReentrantReleaseTarget as a target re-enters release() from its receive(); assert total ETH out == total ETH in (inner call must hit zeroed accounting and revert or no-op; outer completes) - checks-effects or nonReentrant, either way the money invariant holds
- failure isolation: RejectingTarget as ONE release target - pin the chosen semantics and assert the money-safety property: either the whole release reverts atomically (funds stay, retryable) or the healthy leg pays and the rejecting leg's funds remain accounted - AND a rejecting escrow (EthRejector wired at pad) blocks ONLY pad.claimFor(escrow), never other accounts' claims (existing launchpad doctrine, re-proven with our contract in the slot)
- immutability negative checks: no owner/set/withdraw/pause/upgrade fragments in the interface; raw calls to guessed admin selectors do not change targets or splits

- BLOCKER B - SphereRewards puller-authorization model (implied by spec section 8: 'later contracts PULL per their own accounting' but the contract must be FINAL at phase 0 with zero admin): who may pull, and how a phase-2 staking contract gets authorized without an admin function, is undecided (options: immutable controller address, permissionless pull-to-registered-immutable-target, or pure accumulate-only with pull deferred to a phase-2 contract that is the immutable drain target). Blocks ONLY the pull-accounting describe block in SphereRewards.test.ts (written with this.skip() + TODO naming this blocker). All receive/accumulate/no-forwarding/immutability/claimFor cases writable and runnable TODAY - and they are the spec's actual phase-0 gate.
- Section-10 #3, #4, #5, #6, #7, #10 (Safe buyback %, burn/redistribute split, epoch length, per-token cap, staking multiplier, Merkle dispute window): block NOTHING here - they parameterize SphereBuyback/SphereDistributor/SphereStaking (phase 1-2 contracts), not SphereCreatorEscrow or SphereRewards.

## 8. Ryzyka nieporozumien uzytkownika + mitygacje

- **'Dlaczego moj drop jest mniejszy, niz sugeruje wolumen' - cap per tworca (nagroda <= wklad netto do fee protokolu), sqrt(wolumen), cap 10% na token i mnoznik unikalnych traderow sprawiaja, ze liczba na wyplacie nie wynika wprost z wolumenu, ktory tworca widzi na boardzie.** -> Nie pokazywac surowego wolumenu jako podstawy nagrody nigdzie w UI nagrod - pokazywac 'reward score' z rozwijanym breakdownem (wolumen zaliczony -> po limicie oplat -> po korekcie wielkosci -> mnoznik traderow -> boost stakingu), kazdy krok jedna linijka. Stale zdanie o capie przy kazdej liczbie nagrod. Licznik unikalnych traderow z progami widoczny per token ('43 traders = 1.0x; 100+ = 1.2x'), zeby tworca wiedzial, co realnie podnosi score.
- **'Dlaczego buyback nie kupil dzisiaj' - w fazie 0 transze sa uznaniowe (polityka Safe), a dodatkowo celowo losowany jest timing i wielkosc (anty-MEV, sekcja 5), wiec zadnej regularnosci nie wolno obiecac.** -> Dwuklasowe plakietki (Enforced by contract vs Team-run, on-chain) na kazdej pozycji dashboardu; zadnego kalendarza ani 'next buyback' w UI; komunikowac wylacznie fakty historyczne ('ostatnia transza: 2 dni temu, 0.4 ETH') i sume narastajaco. Gdy w fazie 2 wejdzie modul wymuszajacy - dopiero wtedy zmienic plakietke na gwarancje i zrobic z tego komunikat produktowy.
- **'Skoro buyback, to czemu nie wszystko spalone' - split 20/80 (burn/pula nagrod) jest nieintuicyjny; czesc userow uzna 80% za 'niedopalone', inni odwrotnie - ze burn to scam, bo totalSupply na explorerze dalej 1B.** -> Jeden segmentowy pasek 20/80 na dashboardzie i w panelu boardu (konwencja paskow z modalu launchu) z podpisem 'spalone na zawsze / pula nagrod tworcow'; przy burnie zawsze '0xdEaD' z linkiem do explorera i licznikiem 'wylaczone z obiegu'. Jedno zdanie 'dlaczego nie 100% burn': 'Kupione SPHERE placi tworcom - to oni utrzymuja obrot' (arg. z sekcji 5).
- **Szacunek w trakcie tygodnia rozni sie od finalnej wyplaty (wagi, capy i wykluczenia wash-tradingu naliczane przy snapshot'cie po settle+harvest) - tworca widzi 500 SPHERE w srode rano, dostaje 380 wieczorem i czuje sie okradziony.** -> Kazda liczba przed finalizacja z twardym labelem 'estimate/szacunkowo' i tooltipem 'final after the week closes'; stan 'Calculating (up to 24h)' miedzy koncem tygodnia a publikacja; po finalizacji breakdown pokazuje, ktory krok obnizyl liczbe. W pierwszych 2-4 tygodniach shadow (bez wyplat) wyraznie 'Preview season' - to zarazem trening oczekiwan.
- **'Odebralem ETH, czemu nie dostalem SPHERE' (i odwrotnie) - dwa rozne systemy wyplat: istniejacy Claim ETH (launchpad+hook+vault, do 3 podpisow) i nowy Claim SPHERE (distributor, 1 podpis), oba w tym samym panelu My tokens.** -> Dwa osobne pille, rozne kolory i ikony (zielony ETH z NATIVE_SYMBOL vs brandowy z logo SPHERE), nigdy nie sumowane w jedna kwote; osobne toasty. Opcjonalnie jeden przycisk 'Claim all' ktory wykonuje oba flow sekwencyjnie - wzorzec claimAllMine juz obsluguje petle wielu transakcji z jednym statusem busy.
